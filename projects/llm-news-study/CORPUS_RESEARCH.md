## Research question and user clarification

Can a free collector obtain sufficiently relevant, varied, timestamped news for a defensible study of Alibaba's **NYSE-listed BABA ADSs**, including historical lookback windows? A high extraction success rate cannot establish this: independent reporting, missing dates, entity mistakes, repeated events, and later revisions could all invalidate the intended claim.

The user selected BABA rather than the Hong Kong listing. The [issuer's investor FAQ](https://www.alibabagroup.com/en-US/faqs-investor-information) identifies NYSE BABA as American Depositary Shares, each representing eight ordinary shares, and Hong Kong codes 9988/89988 as ordinary-share listings. The future target uses the US security/calendar. News about Hong Kong financing or Chinese operations can still concern the same issuer; do not discard it merely because it mentions 9988. Do not substitute a Hong Kong return label for BABA.

The user's intended later experiment is a historical prediction date (example: a Friday in 2025), supplying preceding news over alternative windows such as 5/10/20 days. Calendar versus trading days, exact decision time, return interval, date range, and exact model remain unresolved. These are examples, not approved experimental defaults. A pretraining cutoff plus disabled browsing does not certify absence of memorization or establish historical availability of later-downloaded text. Preserve these distinct limitations; keep model work outside this collector task.

## Corpus design recommendation

1. Retain issuer announcements and appropriate SEC disclosures as primary material, explicitly marked as issuer-originated. For foreign issuers investigate 6-K/20-F; the current 8-K-only adapter is insufficient for BABA.
2. Add independent reporting through permitted feeds and broad discovery. An aggregator is a discovery service, not an original publisher or a license to retrieve every linked page. Audit original publishers and content families; do not inflate diversity with syndicated copies.
3. Collect sector and macroeconomic context under separate relevance rules. For BABA, research Chinese consumption/e-commerce/cloud policy, competitors and US-China restrictions, as well as US monetary context. A company-name filter will miss meaningful context. Predefine topic/issuer links and retain the evidence; do not declare every general market story relevant to BABA.
4. Preserve headline-only records when bodies are unavailable; distinguish summaries, feed bodies, full pages and filings. Compare text variants later without silently changing the underlying sample.
5. Assess sources by coverage, time precision, access/revision evidence, relevance, independent origins, duplicate/event concentration and text faithfulness. Adding famous domains without accessible, permitted, timestamped material does not solve the research problem.

## Headline scope and account-free expansion, 2026-10-25

**Question → methods evidence → limitation → decision:** should we include only ticker headlines or all market news? Rechecked [Lopez-Lira/Tang v6, section 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.07619v6) and [Ding et al., sections 3.5–3.6/tables 3–4](https://aclanthology.org/D14-1148.pdf), reading depth relevant sections checked. The former uses strongly company-related headlines, both announcements and reporting, with repeated-event and pure price-move controls; the latter explicitly compares title/body and company/sector/all-news inputs. Those designs do not establish that arbitrary market news improves a BABA forecast. Preserve a company-focused sample and a separately selectable broader-context sample; do not claim to reproduce commercial relevance scores or predictive results. Next check is relevance review and actual date/event coverage, before model evaluation.
