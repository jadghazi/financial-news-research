## Covered invariants

- A headline may qualify before its body; later body retrieval cannot enter an earlier strict export.
- A current download carrying an old publication date cannot enter a strict historical cutoff.
- Future publication times, including date-only future values, are quarantined.
- Adding later revisions/copies does not alter previously eligible record selection. Repeated unchanged headline observations retain their first observation.
- Changing current company calendar configuration cannot rewrite session assignment in an older saved record.
- Missing offsets and date-only timestamps do not acquire invented UTC instants.
- Strict queries require an aware cutoff. Unverified archive replay is explicitly refused.
- Exports exclude source sentiment/reasoning and human annotations, record truncation, preserve manifests and refuse overwriting.
- Exchange cases cover pre-open, exact close, weekend, holiday, early close, both DST transitions and the exceptional 2025-01-09 US market closure.
- Source discoveries are committed per response, so a later metadata-request failure cannot erase already returned items. Cancellation after discovery preserves pending records and first-seen times on resume.
- Controlled feed-to-export cases account for every unique discovered URL, per-job caps, 403 failures, 429 retries and robots denial. Repeated URLs are explicitly logged rather than silently reclassified.
- Configured general-news feeds do not become company-specific merely because they were added under a ticker.
- Substantial publisher-provided RSS bodies can be processed without requesting a blocked webpage; original discovery responses remain preserved.
- SEC fixtures cover historical submission-file traversal, item metadata, separate filing-date/acceptance semantics and actionable missing-contact behavior.
- Archive checksums detect changed bytes; an independently opened backup recovers the original bytes.
- UI tests visit all seven pages, save a review annotation and check the offline example. CLI tests exercise demo, all three text variants, manifests and verification. Parquet round-trip and CSV formula escaping are tested.

## Limits and consciously deferred parts

- No account-bearing source was activated. SEC live collection still needs the user's actual contact identity and reachable source access. Its adapter is fixture-tested, not claimed live-verified. Primary filing extraction does not automatically retrieve exhibits.
- GDELT remains optional and does not carry a completeness guarantee. Prior historical probing was inconclusive; returned indexing times are not relabelled as publication times. No source can promise retrieval of every news article.
- Wayback/Common Crawl reconstruction is **not implemented**. A strict historical query therefore cannot use later downloads merely because a publisher date is old. `archived_replay` is rejected, not silently downgraded.
- Fundus and browser rendering were not required by the three tested source paths and were not installed/integrated. The implemented routes use Trafilatura plus a deterministic structural parser. This is not a measured claim of superiority over other extractors.
- Near-duplicate MinHash clustering, verified effective-date ticker-history resolution, statistical relevance estimates, arbitrary PDF extraction, full exchange coverage and a 60-100-document extractor benchmark remain future quality extensions. Current deduplication is exact normalized text, preserving original copies.
- The manual review page provides an auditable convenience queue; it does not calculate population precision from an unrepresentative sample.
- There is no measured guarantee for recovery from every hardware/filesystem failure, DNS rebinding resistance, every source's licensing or long unattended deployments. The app is local, bounds URLs/requests/storage, preserves failure evidence and is not exposed publicly.
- SQLite automatically uses rollback journaling on this installed older SQLite build; it enables WAL only for the documented fixed-version ranges. This avoids relying on the affected WAL race while retaining short transactions and a single ingestion worker.
