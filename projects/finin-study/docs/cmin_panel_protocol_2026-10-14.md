# CMIN company-panel protocol, frozen before fitting

The user authorized building a public pipeline for FININ-related headline interactions, temporal decay and final fusion on October 14, 2026. This version builds the baseline and the controls; it does not run a professor-task ablation sweep. It is an independent adaptation, with conditional date-based news timing, not an author empirical reproduction or trading system.

Configuration: `configs/cmin_panel_v1.json`. Data and all new output: `artifacts/cmin_panel_2026-10-14_v1/`. Existing experiments remain intact. The 12-company cohort is determined from the pinned release's ticker inventory by a content-independent SHA256 rule. Neither returns nor news coverage selects stocks. Five input sessions and six headlines per arrival session are declared student compute choices; CMIN used five input days and a 20-headline cap. Frozen actual RoBERTa CLS and finance TinyBERT sentiment are reused offline, with no new model downloads; max64tokens is an explicit truncation budget. Company descriptions use only the literal ticker, avoiding retrospective corporate descriptions.

## Information timeline

At exchange close d, five consecutive XNYS sessions' price features and eligible news buckets predict adjusted-close[q] > adjusted-close[d], where q is the immediately next exchange session. Ties are DOWN; no filtering by future return magnitude. Numeric inputs: previous adjusted-close return, current Open/Close−1, High/Close−1, Low/Close−1, High/Low−1 and log(1+volume). Raw summaries and same-row processed returns are not features/targets. Adjusted-price vintages and retrospective large-cap selection remain limitations.

Source times lack timezone. News dated D is provisionally available at D+2 00:00UTC, assigned once to its first eligible exchange close; this delays after-close recap articles beyond the return they describe. Exact historical availability is unverified. Record both source date/time/link and the proxy. Age hours are relative to source-date UTC midnight, with day-level uncertainty; they cannot establish intraday diffusion. Empty-news dates remain in every arm. Per-company repeated normalized titles retain the earliest dated record; retained exact title hashes identify cross-company duplicates. Inspect differing arrival dates and coverage before fitting; partial or revised archives cannot be cured by a delay.

## Fixed evaluation and budget

Fold0: target dates before2020-01-01 train; Jan–Apr2020 validation; May–Aug2020 evaluation. Fold1: targets before2020-09-01 train; Sep–Dec2020 validation; Jan–Apr2021 evaluation. For each fold, purge train/validation labels unavailable strictly before the next block's first forecast cutoff. Dates and samples match all arms; every company uses the same boundaries. Later folds may train on earlier evaluation dates under this fixed chronological schedule.

Numerical mean/std and text centering/global RMS use unique training-history company-sessions and their selected text only. No fit uses evaluation labels. Checkpoint selection: lowest raw validation BCE over12epochs. Constant-probability fallback is selected from validation and reported separately from raw neural outputs; it must not disguise collapse. Shared CPU models: prices only, prices+mean news, FININ joint interaction/query weighting; two seeds17/42 in two folds =12fits maximum. Width16, dropout.2, AdamW learning rate.0003/weight decay.01, batch64, no search. Cumulative neural-time ceiling2,400seconds checked at epoch boundaries; incomplete attempts retain failure evidence and are not automatically retried. At most80,000 unique frozen-feature items.

References: fixed50/50, training prevalence, and fixed-L2 logistic prices plus company identity. Primary outcome is paired held-out log loss, equal company-day weight, averaging seed losses rather than ensembled probabilities. Practical improvement criterion: at least.005 lower log loss, a negative conditional95%paired date-block interval, and favorable mean differences in both periods. This criterion is a declared project choice, not a universal significance standard. Secondary outputs: MCC, balanced accuracy, class recalls, AUC, Brier and probability dispersion. Two seeds/two periods give limited diagnostic evidence. Five-date blocks retain stocks together; intervals condition on fits and omit model-fitting uncertainty. Report parameter counts as module-level upper bounds with inactive columns, rather than claiming matched active capacity. The time ceiling covers the fitting engine, with at most one-epoch overshoot; acquisition, encoding and post-fit evaluation are separate. Each completed epoch's metrics, predictions and selected state are retained before a stop, and initial weights are saved before optimization.

September–December2021 outcomes and model evaluations remain excluded. Raw source files include that period and are audited; it is a publicly studied benchmark, not globally unseen data. Preserve it from this project's selection. No additional final fits are included in this12-fit authorization. If news/attention does not improve this bounded study, report that result and stop automatic tuning/dataset changes. A functioning computation is separate from useful forecasting.

## Professor-task controls

`make_model` exposes `no_interaction`, `no_query`, `no_sentiment`, `news_only`, `no_direct_market`, `pooled_uniform`, and `pooled_decay`, in addition to baseline arms. They are not run by the default configuration. `news_only` removes prices from both query and predictor; `no_direct_market` independently removes the direct market representation from the final MLP while keeping market-conditioned news weighting. `PanelDataset(news_age_sessions=(lower,upper))` restricts news ages since arrival while preserving the complete price history; `text_available=False` removes semantic text while retaining sentiment/coverage. Decay requires pooling and a declared positive half-life; fixed weights on concatenated daily slots are absorbable by the MLP. Use date-relative proxy age labels honestly.

Every selected checkpoint saves traces for the first evaluation row per company, selected without inspecting outcomes: headline projection/fusion, self-attention, market-query scores/weights, daily summaries and actual MLP input. In concat mode, the final vector is all five market representations followed by all five weighted news summaries. Sentiment enters headline fusion earlier, while weights shape summaries. Retrained matched ablations can assess predictive contribution; attention diagrams or fitted decay weights alone do not establish causal influence or information diffusion.

## Commands

Use `.venv/Scripts/python.exe scripts/16_cmin_panel.py acquire`, then `prepare`, `cache`, `run`, `inspect`. Stages have explicit side effects; `acquire` fetches only the frozen US cohort and verifies remote Git blob identities. `prepare` generates development-only arrays and audits. `cache` computes pinned offline features once. `run` fits only the frozen baseline study and retains initial/selected/final checkpoints, histories, predictions, source snapshots and environment. Existing completed results are verified for a compatible resume; incomplete fits are retained, never silently replaced. Before reporting, independent review must inspect actual artifacts and recompute important metrics.

Later explicitly designed studies can use `run --config NEW_CONFIG --data-directory AUDITED_COHORT --study-directory NEW_OUTPUT --protocol NEW_PROTOCOL`. This reuses the frozen feature cache and refuses changes to cohort geometry/timing or overwriting the baseline. `news_age_sessions` and `comparison_pairs` are explicit optional configuration fields; `sentiment_only` removes headline vectors while retaining sentiment/coverage. Such analyses on these already inspected development periods are exploratory; this interface does not automatically run them or open the reserved outcomes.
