# Public-data research decision — 2026-10-14

**Decision:** prioritize CMIN-US for a bounded, independent FININ-inspired company-day study. Public adaptation is feasible; no ready, independently verified empirical reproduction or useful forecasting baseline has been established. This replaces the licensed-data-first priority in the October 13 feasibility note, following the student's explicit permission to change papers, datasets and pipelines while preserving the research questions.

**Scope executed:** primary-paper/release/code inspection and small source samples; literature investigation and independent skeptical review. No training, language-model fine-tuning, broad tuning, bulk dataset download or changes to existing models/experiments. Prior artifacts remain intact. Reviewer/researcher conduct was read-only; parent permissions did not enforce read-only access.

## Why CMIN-US

[Luo et al., ACL 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.679/) studies headline-enhanced next-step stock direction. Its [author dataset](https://github.com/BigRoddy/CMIN-Dataset) provides 110 US companies, Yahoo news and prices, with stated coverage 2018–2021. Appendix A/Table 3 specifies chronological training through April 2021, development May–August, and test September–December. Section 5.1.4 uses five input days and up to 20 headlines/day. These are useful reference choices, not obligations for the new study.

The recommendation is based on released files as well as the paper. At commit `3f571a53b81d8f3ea7b3afb9877bdbdbeae54c97`, directory metadata lists 110 raw-news files totaling **633,557,930 bytes**, 110 raw-price files totaling **7,552,468 bytes**, and processed-price files totaling 13,845,888 bytes. These totals are remote inventory sizes, not downloaded amounts. Download only the US subset later; cloning the entire repository would also retrieve the Chinese data/history.

Two 16 KiB news prefixes contain 32 complete AAPL stories over two dates and 30 ABBV stories over 13 dates. Raw TSV fields are `date,time,ticker,name,title,summary,link`. Multiple separate stories per company-day are therefore directly verified. Full AAPL raw prices have 1,008 sessions, 2018-01-02–2021-12-31, with OHLC, adjusted close and volume. Prefixes do **not** prove full-period headline completeness, exact company relevance or deduplication quality.

Use **raw** prices to define features and labels. The processed AAPL first numeric field is the adjusted return ending on that row's date, not a next-session target. Processed AAPL has 1,002 rows, ending December 23; the omissions require explanation only if reproducing author preprocessing. They do not establish outcome filtering. The [owner's normalization explanation](https://github.com/BigRoddy/CMIN-Dataset/issues/1) is resolved but distinguishes the Chinese data; it should not be copied indiscriminately to US prices.

**Timing remains conditional:** news times lack a timezone. One listed AAPL time differs from the clock-like URL suffix, potentially reflecting updates or source conventions. ABBV contains an after-close return recap. Such a story cannot predict the return it already describes. A delayed, daily experiment is feasible if these limitations are declared; exact intraday diffusion or historically executable trading is unsupported.

The published model reports 53.43% accuracy and MCC 0.046 on CMIN-US (Table 1). That is modest association, not proof of gains over an appropriate majority/prior reference. Original training code and saved predictions were not verified. The author release contains data, not a verified runnable CMIN implementation. We should call the project **a FININ-inspired study using CMIN-US**, not a FININ/CMIN empirical reproduction. Keeping a compact existing architecture avoids rebuilding the paper's additional stock-correlation/transfer-entropy machinery.

Additional primary checks: [CausalStock, NeurIPS 2024](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/54d689d58fe54c92aee2d732fc49fca8-Abstract-Conference.html) also uses CMIN-US. For CAMEF/GS-FUSE, direct inspection of pinned loaders found default `split_by_time=False`; even enabling chronological sorting assigns training first 60%, **test next 20%, validation last 20%**. This requires correction before a prospective train/validation/test claim. Pins: CAMEF `bd31b0cc327081f309d3f1510efa8855e351bb75`, GS-FUSE `2bb1fa75a032ea6efef52e118e6ba109ee449a5f`. Paper descriptions alone do not establish the released behavior. FinMultiTime's linked GitHub implementation resolved to an unrelated deep graph clustering library; no forecasting code was verified.

## Bounded next step — proposed, not executed

1. **One data audit.** Use a fixed, outcome-independent US cohort, then assess period coverage, company relevance, source clocks, repeated/updated stories, exchange sessions, corporate actions and headline counts. Keep empty-news sessions. Preserve raw snapshots and manifests. A small cohort checks parsing; it must not become a performance-selected stock subset. The full US news release is about 604 MiB, substantially smaller than multi-gigabyte alternatives.
2. **Freeze the information timeline and protocol.** Predict next-session adjusted-close direction at the current session's close, using only current/past prices and eligible prior news. Provisional conservative date proxy: a story dated D becomes eligible at 00:00 UTC on D+2; audit this rule and declare lag sensitivity before fitting. This does not certify historical availability. Split all companies on identical chronological boundaries and purge training labels that cross boundaries. Retrospective large-cap selection and adjusted-price vintages remain limitations. Existing inspected SPNews evaluation dates are not a fresh holdout.
3. **One compact baseline study.** Share a single model across companies; frozen headline features computed once; compact learned projection and predictor. Compare constant 50/50 and training-prior probabilities, a lightweight price reference, and three small trainable arms: price-only, prices plus mean headlines, and prices plus separate headline interaction/market-query aggregation. Match dates, price history, headline selection and selection budgets. Two earlier chronological development windows × three arms × two seeds gives a proposed ceiling of **12 small neural fits**, with a fixed configuration and no broad search. Reserve the published late-2021 test interval until the study is frozen; opening it would be a separate declared final evaluation, not free additional tuning.
4. **Decide once.** Check optimization and information integrity separately from forecasting. Primary metric: paired held-out log loss; secondary MCC, balanced accuracy, calibration and class-specific results. Fix the practically meaningful effect criterion before new fits. Estimate uncertainty over date blocks, retaining all companies together; company-days and seeds are not independent market histories. Constant-class output can be a legitimate null result: do not force both directions or treat two-class prediction as proof of learning. If the capped study shows no news benefit, retain and report that finding, without automatically switching datasets/models.

## How it addresses the professor's questions

| Question | Controlled comparison and useful output |
|---|---|
| Headline relationships/attention | Hold prices and text fixed; compare mean pooling, interaction attention, market-query weighting and their combination. Inspect whether attention links duplicates, contradictory sentiment or related events. Saved attention maps are diagnostics; retrained matched comparisons establish predictive contribution, not causation. |
| Importance decay | Keep price history fixed. Compare news age bins over a predeclared five-session history, and uniform versus pooled explicit age weighting. Plot held-out prediction benefit by news age with date-block uncertainty. A fitted weight curve alone cannot establish diffusion; daily uncertain dates support coarse predictive-age analysis. Use pooling so fixed day weights cannot simply be absorbed by the final MLP's independent day-slot parameters. |
| Final fusion/MLP contributions | Trace the actual inputs, then remove or add headlines, sentiment and market-conditioned weighting separately with retraining. Existing FININ-style sentiment enters headline fusion earlier; weights shape summaries. They are not automatically independent columns of the final MLP. Removing a direct price path while retaining prices in the query does not remove price information. |

A reliable project here means auditable measurement and an interpretable positive, negative or inconclusive result. It does not require assuming the professor's hypotheses are true or recovering a published score. The expanded company panel improves the opportunity to study company-relevant news; it does not guarantee forecasting signal or create thousands of independent market periods.

## Evidence retained and independent review

Source inventory, pinned URLs, bounded sample hashes, failed/corrected requests and schema audit: `artifacts/feasibility/public_reference_2026-10-14/`. The news prefixes are partial samples, not complete CSV releases. Independent review directly inspected these samples and the CMIN paper, supported conditional daily-study feasibility, and identified timezone, processed-label alignment, coverage and correlated-date uncertainty as remaining issues. No positive forecasting finding was made.

Prior forecasting evidence remains `reports/public_headline_pipeline_2026-10-13.md`: full FININ log loss 0.7539 versus matched no-news 0.7068; no repeatable overall gain under that configuration. Earlier full models predicted DOWN throughout, later models both directions. Those failures motivate a better research population and a strict budget; they do not demonstrate universal headline or attention failure.
